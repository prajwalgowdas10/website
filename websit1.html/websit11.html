Prepration of DOSA

The exact single inventor of the dosa is unknown, as it evolved as an ancient regional food in South India. Food historians trace its roots to two primary historical claims: early soft variants mentioned in ancient Tamil Sangam literature around the 1st century CE, and a crispier version recorded as dosaka in the 12th-century Sanskrit text Manasollasa compiled by King Someshvara III in present-day Karnataka
